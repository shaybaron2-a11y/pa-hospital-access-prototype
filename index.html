Spatial inequality in hospital access

This interactive dashboard compares all Pennsylvania counties using ACS population and vulnerability estimates, CMS hospital records, Census TIGERweb county boundaries, and Census Geocoder coordinates.

Interpretation boundary: official inputs do not make the derived score a validated clinical or policy model. Distances are straight-line distances from county boundary centroids, not drive times.
